How we work

Four steps.No shortcuts.

01 / 04

Wash & decon

Foam pre-soak, two-bucket hand wash, iron remover and clay. The paint gets truly clean before anything touches it.

02 / 04

Correct

Machine polishing levels out swirls, haze and light scratches so the clear coat reflects like glass again.

FAQ

Goodquestions.

How long does a full detail take?

A maintenance wash takes a few hours. Correction and coating work usually takes one to three days, depending on the car and the condition of the paint.

Ceramic coating or wax — what's the difference?

Wax sits on top of the paint and wears off in weeks. A ceramic coating bonds to the clear coat and lasts much longer, with stronger water beading and easier washing. We'll recommend what fits how you use the car.

Do I need an appointment?

Yes — we book one car at a time so nothing gets rushed. Send a request below or call, and we'll find a slot.

Do you work on trucks, SUVs and classics?

Yes. Larger vehicles and older single-stage paint just need a slightly different plan and price, which we'll confirm after a look.

How should I wash the car afterward?

Gently: a pH-neutral soap, clean mitts and no automatic brushes. You'll get a short aftercare guide with every protection package.
